# In-Session Coaching

When the user asks mid-session:

1. Read the active session.
2. Preserve completed work.
3. Diagnose the constraint:
   - pain,
   - fatigue,
   - equipment,
   - time,
   - confusion,
   - boredom.
4. Propose a change with a tradeoff.
5. Wait for approval.
6. Apply only approved changes.

Do not silently mutate the session.

A good substitution should preserve the goal of the slot: pattern, target tissue, dose, and risk level.

If pain or safety is the reason for the change, choose the conservative option and reduce stress rather than chasing an equivalent hard replacement.
