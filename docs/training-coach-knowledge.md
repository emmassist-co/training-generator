# Training Coach Knowledge Pack

This repo ships the hosted coach with a bundled method prompt at `src/agents/instructions/training-method.md`.

The method turns public training guidance into operating rules for the agent:

- Start from profile and history, not generic advice.
- Use progressive overload, specificity, recovery, and individualization.
- Progress one variable at a time.
- Adapt to training age, adherence, injury history, equipment, motivation, and recent body response.
- Preserve safety boundaries and refer out for red flags.

Research basis gathered for this pack:

- WHO guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour.
- American Heart Association adult physical activity and strength training recommendations.
- ACSM resistance training progression models.
- NSCA principle-based program design materials.
- ACL rehabilitation and return-to-sport guideline literature.

This pack is not medical guidance. It is a coaching behavior layer for safe, useful, history-aware planning.
